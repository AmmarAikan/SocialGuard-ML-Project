Project Scope

Business ProblemDetect fake accounts that may be used for phishing, spam, impersonation, social engineering, or misinformation.
ML ObjectiveBinary classification: Real profile vs Fake profile using account-level and aggregated activity-level signals.
Deployment ScopeUse either a saved dataset row or custom user-entered values, then compare predictions across six trained algorithms.

Enter the most important profile and activity values. The app automatically fills remaining model features with training-set medians/modes and computes engineered features such as engagement rate and profile signal score.
